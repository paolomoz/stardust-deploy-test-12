# PRODUCT — apple.com (current state)

**Register:** brand (marketing/landing surface — a product showcase, not a tool).

## Users
Consumers and prospective buyers across Apple's product lines (iPhone, Mac,
iPad, Watch, services) plus segment audiences routed from the footer (Business,
Education, Healthcare, Government). The home page is a routing surface: it does
not sell on the page, it directs to product pages and the store.

## Product Purpose
Present the current product lineup and seasonal promotions as a curated grid of
self-contained tiles, each routing to "Learn more" (the product story) or "Buy"
(the store). The home page's job is hierarchy and routing, not conversion copy.

## Brand Personality
Confident, terse, product-forward. Headlines are the product name itself
("iPhone", "MacBook Air", "Apple Card"); the benefit is one declarative line
("Now supercharged by M5.", "Mac and iPad. Major in any field."). Almost no
adjectives. The product photography does the persuading; type stays out of its
way. Generous negative space, precise alignment, restrained palette. Motion on
the live site is subtle (image zoom on hover, gallery autoplay) — the brand
reads as *quiet precision*, not spectacle, on the home grid (the cinematic
work lives on the individual product pages).

## Anti-references
- Dense feature-list marketing pages.
- Loud gradient-heavy SaaS landing pages.
- Stock photography. Apple uses only first-party product renders/photography.

## Design Principles
1. **The product is the hero.** Full-bleed photography; type is a caption to it.
2. **One idea per tile.** Name + one benefit line + one or two links. Never more.
3. **Restraint as signature.** Near-black on white/light-gray, one blue accent,
   one pill shape. No decorative color.
4. **Hierarchy by scale, not ornament.** 56px hero headers, 40px tile headers,
   driven by SF Pro Display semibold; the grid does the rest.

# PRODUCT — apple.com redesign target

**Register:** brand. A product showcase and routing surface, not a tool.

## Users
Consumers and prospective buyers across Apple's lineup (iPhone, Mac, iPad,
Watch, services). The home page edits and routes: it ranks the lineup and sends
visitors to product stories ("Learn more") or the store ("Buy"/"Shop").

## Product Purpose
Present the current lineup and seasonal promotions as an edited sequence of
self-contained product moments. The home page's job is hierarchy and routing,
carried by photography and type, not by feature copy.

## Brand Personality
Confident, terse, product-forward. The product is the hero; type is its caption.
Restraint as signature: near-black on white and light gray, one functional blue,
one pill shape. Hierarchy by scale, not ornament. Quiet precision over spectacle.
Traits (for register selection): `brand`, `display-typography-signature`,
`modular-catalogue`, `product`.

## Anti-references
- Dense feature-list marketing pages.
- Loud gradient-heavy SaaS landing pages.
- Stock photography (Apple uses only first-party product imagery).

## Design Principles
1. The product is the hero; type is a caption to it.
2. One idea per section: name + one benefit line + one or two actions.
3. Restraint as signature; color reserved for the photography.
4. Hierarchy by scale, driven by SF Pro Display.

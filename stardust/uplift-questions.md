# "What if…" candidates — https://dala.craftedbygc.com/

## Picked

### Variant C · Signature-gesture extension (#4)
What if: "What if the generative constellation stopped being a hero backdrop and became the brand's structural voice — threading from the first viewport through every section and resolving in the footer wordmark?"
Cinematic register: arrival
Evidence: `_brand-extraction.json#motifs.patterns` shows `generative-particle-hero` ("a black-background particle/constellation cloud behind a giant typographic headline") present at the hero only; the remaining sections collapse to `manifesto-longform` prose with no recurring motif. The constellation is the literal product thesis (scattered fragments → shared knowledge) yet it is used exactly once.
Motion bet: arrival choreography — hero particles parallax upward, manifesto sections rise and stagger in, count-style reveals on the mission band, and the constellation's points seed section dividers and a footer wordmark wipe. Motion is the identity; IA stays identical to A.

### Variant B · Display-typography amplification (#1)
What if: "What if type *was* the interface — a strict modular scale and the full PP Neue Montreal weight range (200→600) the site owns but never exercises, doing all the structural work?"
Captured trait amplified: display-typography signature (single private typeface carrying the whole brand)
Evidence: `_brand-extraction.json#type` — one family (PP Neue Montreal) does display + body + UI; three weights captured (200/400/600) but headings render at weight `[400]` only; `scaleAudit.kind = "ad-hoc"`. The brand's defining material is type, yet two-thirds of its weight range and any scale discipline go unused.
Composition bet: rebuild the page on a strict modular scale (directly retiring tension `T-scale`), introduce weight contrast (Light 200 atmospheric body vs SemiBold 600 structural heads), add numbered section eyebrows, and let oversized type — not imagery — be the layout. Composition/typography axis, static motion.

## Disqualified

- **Photography re-foregrounding (#2)** — disqualified because the brand deliberately avoids photography (anti-reference: "no stock illustration"; hero is generative abstraction, not a photograph). The only captured photography is 3 founder portraits + 5 decorative investor PNGs; amplifying it would fight the brand and expose the trust-strip cliché (improvement #4).
- **Live-data promotion (#3)** — disqualified because the site is a pre-launch marketing/manifesto surface with no operational/real-time signals to promote. Nothing live to fake.
- **Audience-routing reframe (#7)** — disqualified because the above-the-fold region carries a single CTA verb ("Request Access"), not the >4 distinct CTA verbs the trigger requires. Multi-audience content (hires, investors) sits below the fold by design, not compressed into the hero.
- **Motif vocabulary swap (#8)** — disqualified because the page runs on essentially one composition primitive (manifesto-longform prose); the slider and logo strip appear once each. No second primitive substantial enough to promote to the default.

## Considered but not picked

- **Color-ladder re-weighting (#6)** — real trigger: the lone accent (purple #8052ff) is used in <10 of ~6000 color occurrences, well under the 10% threshold, so a reserved shade exists to lift. Not picked because promoting purple to a primary ground would erode the brand's load-bearing "one accent, used sparingly" restraint discipline; the differentiation B/C is better served on the type and motion axes.
- **Voice-register pivot (#5)** — mild trigger (aspirational-manifesto tone dominant vs. functional product tone in the margins). Not picked: foregrounding the functional tone would make C *less* cinematic, not more, and B/C already differentiate cleanly by axis (type vs motion) without a voice move.

# Direction — Linear uplift (presales, 3 variants)

Brand-faithful Mode A. Palette pinned to the captured surface
(`#08090a` ground, `#f7f8f8` text, `#5e6ad2` primary, `#e4f222` lime,
hairline `#23252a`). Typography pinned to Inter Variable + Berkeley
Mono. No invented colors or fonts. IA priority preserved: hero claim +
primary CTA first viewport; the six capability pillars and closing band
retained.

## Cinematic register (variant C)

`live-systems` — selected from PRODUCT.md Brand Personality
(`operationally-transparent` / `data-led` / `dashboard-register`).
Tie-break vs `kinetic-grid` (the `product`/`SaaS` match) resolved to
`live-systems` because its *refuses* clause (no animated headings —
"the data is the choreography") aligns with Linear's craft-through-
restraint principle, and operational liveness is Linear's most
brand-true claim.

## Variant A — Faithful + improvements

Role: risk-averse green-light. "Yes, that's us, with the obvious fixes."
Composition: same as captured — hero claim + product mock, six capability
pillars, closing band.
Motion: static (no cinematic layer).
Improvements applied (from uplift-improvements.md):
1. Re-weight the six equal pillars into a hierarchy (1–2 lead pillars
   carry the section; the rest demote to a compact rail).
2. Lift muted text off the `#62666d` floor; supporting copy moves to
   `#8a8f98`+ for WCAG AA on `#08090a`.
3. Replace the cliché pastel gradient display text with solid
   near-white + a single lime accent word; keep it recognisably Linear.
4. Tighten the dated "dark-SaaS template" read with sharper section
   markers (Berkeley Mono eyebrows + index numerals).
5. Keep the product mock but give it more share-of-canvas.

## Variant B — What if we amplified the reserved lime?

Role: design-team motivator. The brand's underused capability foregrounded.
What if: "What if the reserved lime `#e4f222` stopped being a tiny
highlight chip and became a primary surface?"
Captured trait amplified: lime accent (`#e4f222`), used in 1–3 chips only.
Evidence: `_brand-extraction.json#palette.accentLime`.
Composition: invert one major surface to lime (a full-bleed manifesto
band and/or the closing CTA on lime with `#08090a` text); lime becomes
the structural accent on section markers and the primary CTA. Palette
unchanged — only proportions shift. Type and IA otherwise as A.
Motion: static (no cinematic layer).

## Variant C — What if motion was part of the identity?

Role: visionary pitch. The brand's third dimension — kinetic.
What if: "What if Linear's operational liveness — issues moving, agents
working, progress counting up — *was* the identity, delivered through
motion instead of a frozen screenshot?"
Cinematic register: `live-systems` (auto-picked).
Captured trait amplified: operational liveness (Live-data promotion) —
the register's natural candidate.
Evidence: `_brand-extraction.json#voice` ("Make product operations
self-driving"); hero mock's Activity feed, issue states, "02 / 145"
counters — all frozen.
Composition: identical IA to A; the bet is motion, not layout. A
hero-adjacent live-operations band: count-up numerals, status-pulse dots
on agent/issue rows, progress fill bars, periodic refresh-sweep across
tiles. Headings stay still.
Motion: cinematic, register `live-systems`. Reduced-motion fallback
neutralises every animation.

# PRODUCT — ElevenLabs (current state)

> Descriptive snapshot of the existing site. This is *what the site is*,
> not the redesign target.

## Register

`brand` — marketing/landing surface. Hero with marketing copy, social
proof, capability grids, pricing nav, and signup CTAs above the fold;
no auth needed to view.

## Product Purpose

ElevenLabs is an AI audio research company and platform. The home page
positions two product surfaces on a shared research foundation:
**ElevenCreative** (a creative suite — ultra-realistic speech, music,
SFX, voices, image & video) and **ElevenAgents** (deploy natural,
human-sounding voice agents in 70+ languages), plus a developer
**API** layer (Text-to-Speech, Speech-to-Text, Music). The throughline
is research credibility translated into shipping products.

## Users

- **Enterprises** — customer-experience and contact-center teams
  deploying voice agents; compliance/guardrails buyers.
- **Creators** — video, music, and audio makers needing realistic
  speech and a full creative editor.
- **Developers** — engineers integrating speech/voice via APIs & SDKs
  (Eleven Flash, Multilingual, v3, Scribe model family).

## Brand Personality

Research-forward, precise, quietly confident. The visual identity is
near-monochrome — warm off-white (#FDFCFC) grounds, black ink,
warm-neutral supporting tones — with a **red accent** (#EB524B ramp)
reserved almost entirely for the waveform / research surfaces. The
distinctive **Waldenburg** display cut (set light, weight 300) carries
the headline register; Inter handles body; Geist Mono sets uppercase
eyebrow labels. The result reads as a serious lab that ships polished
product — not a loud consumer SaaS.

## Anti-references

- Loud, gradient-heavy consumer-AI landing pages (neon purple/cyan).
- Stocky "two people at a laptop" hero photography.
- Centered hero + double primary-colored CTA SaaS template.

## Design Principles (observed)

1. **Restraint as credibility** — monochrome warm palette; color is an
   event, not a default. The red appears only where research/audio
   lives.
2. **Typography is the brand** — Waldenburg light at display sizes is
   the single most recognizable signal.
3. **Capability density** — the page is information-rich: platform
   split, feature grids, model cards, research index. It trusts the
   reader to scan.
4. **Pill geometry** — every interactive affordance is a pill
   (9999px). Black-fill primary, white-outline secondary.

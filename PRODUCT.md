# PRODUCT — Adaline (redesign target)

## Register
`product` — developer / ML-engineering tool. Technical, precise, confident.

## Product Purpose
Adaline is an observability and evals platform for self-improving AI agents.
It turns production traces into behaviors, evals, synthetic data, and verified
improvements a team can approve and ship — "the ML layer between your traces
and your agent diffs."

## Users
AI / ML teams responsible for production-agent reliability — technical
(platform engineers, MTS) and semi-technical (PMs, founders). Used by teams at
Salesforce, DoorDash, HubSpot, Superhuman, Reforge, Discord, McKinsey, Coframe.

## Brand Personality
Organic-growth metaphor over hype ("Never stop learning"); earned-technical
(show the machinery as proof); quiet confidence (sage-on-paper, flat surfaces,
generous whitespace); one theatrical serif beat at the close.

## Design Principles
1. Show the machinery as proof.
2. Keep chrome quiet so data and photography carry the page.
3. Mono for technical labels, grotesque for prose, serif for the display beat.
4. Organic palette — paper, ink, sage, forest — never synthetic.

## Redesign intent (presales)
Three brand-faithful variants. A: faithful + 5 fixes. B: amplify the underused
Instrument Serif into the structural voice. C: make the live telemetry the
kinetic hero (register `live-systems`).

Tensions 6

T-scale

Type scale is ad-hoc

Heading sizes don't follow a single modular ratio (1.6 · 1.5 · 1.33). Direct will need to decide whether the target adopts a modular scale.

Source: _brand-extraction.json § type.scaleAudit
T-color-imbalance

Accent #eb524b used in one context only

#eb524b (accent) appears as text/fill only — never as background/border. It lives almost exclusively on the waveform/research surfaces. Direct must decide: keep as rare signal, expand, or drop.

Source: _brand-extraction.json § palette[].usedAs
T-cta-vocab

Two phrasings for the sales path

"Talk to sales" and "Contact sales" both appear for the same action. Direct should pick one canonical sales CTA.

Source: pages/home.json § ctas
T-cta-vocab

"Learn more" carries every secondary CTA

"Learn more" appears 5× as the generic secondary affordance (creative, agents, about, safety…). Screen-reader and scan-clarity tension — the destination isn't in the label. Direct could differentiate ("See ElevenAgents", "Read the research").

Source: pages/home.json § ctas
T-logo-variants

Single logo variant captured

Only the inline-SVG wordmark was captured. The redesign will likely need monochrome / inverted variants; direct should plan that.

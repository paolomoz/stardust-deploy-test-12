# PRODUCT — ElevenLabs (redesign target)

## Register

`brand` — a marketing/landing surface for an AI audio research company.

## Product purpose

ElevenLabs turns research credibility into shipping products across two
surfaces on one research foundation: **ElevenCreative** (speech, music,
SFX, voices, image & video) and **ElevenAgents** (natural voice agents
in 70+ languages), plus the **ElevenAPI** developer layer. The redesign
keeps this structure and sharpens the path through it.

## Users

- **Enterprises** — CX / contact-center teams deploying voice agents.
- **Creators** — video, music, audio makers needing realistic speech.
- **Developers** — engineers integrating speech/voice via APIs & SDKs.

## Brand personality

Research-forward, precise, quietly confident. Restraint reads as
credibility: a near-monochrome warm palette where colour is an event,
the distinctive Waldenburg light display cut as the headline voice, and
the audio waveform as the one recurring gesture. The redesign honours
this — it does not turn ElevenLabs into a loud consumer-AI page.

## Design principles

1. **Restraint as credibility** — monochrome warm ground; colour is
   intentional, not default.
2. **Typography is the brand** — Waldenburg light at display sizes is
   the single most recognizable signal.
3. **One clear path** — resolve competing CTAs; name destinations.
4. **Pill geometry** — every affordance is a pill (`9999px`).
5. **Sound made visible** — the waveform / gradient-orb system is the
   brand's sensory signature; give it room.

## Anti-references

- Neon purple/cyan gradient consumer-AI landing pages.
- Stock "people at a laptop" hero photography.
- Centered hero + double primary-colored CTA SaaS template.

# "What if…" candidates — https://www.duolingo.com/

### 1 · Display-typography amplification — **QUALIFIED (selected for B)**
**What if Feather Bold extended from modest headings into the page's structural voice?**
Evidence: the captured display family `feather` (Feather Bold, 700) is a genuine proprietary signature — chunky, rounded, lowercase-by-convention — but it tops out at 64px and is used as ordinary section headings. A face this distinctive is the brand's most amplifiable non-color asset.
Best serves: **B** (composition / IA bet — type-as-structure, static).
Natural register for C: kinetic-display.

### 2 · Photography re-foregrounding — **DISQUALIFIED**
Duolingo uses flat character **illustrations**, not photography, and they are already foregrounded at large scale. There is no cropped-to-thumbnail photography to restore. Amplifying "photography" would mean inventing a register the brand doesn't have.

### 3 · Live-data promotion — **DISQUALIFIED**
The marketing homepage exposes no real-time operational signals (no wait times, availability, departures). The brand's gamification numbers (streaks, XP) are *product* data, not live operational state, and aren't present on the captured surface at all — so there's nothing live to promote into the hero.

### 4 · Signature-gesture extension — **QUALIFIED (selected for C)**
**What if the pushable 3D button and Duo the owl stopped being decorations and became a kinetic system?**
Evidence: the captured surface has two defining gestures used only incidentally — the **pushable button** (fill + darker bottom border that compresses on press) and **Duo + the character cast** floating in illustration clusters. Both are the brand's tactile/emotional signature, yet on the marketing page they just sit there.
Best serves: **C** (motion bet — the gesture becomes the brand's third dimension).
Natural register for C: `arrival` (composition-led motion: parallaxing clusters, rising plates, depress-on-press, count-ups).

### 5 · Voice-register pivot — **DISQUALIFIED**
The captured voice is tonally uniform — playful/warm/lowercase throughout, no formal counter-register hiding in the margins to foreground. Nothing to pivot toward.

### 6 · Color-ladder re-weighting — **QUALIFIED (runner-up for B)**
**What if the reserved deep navy `#100F3E` (used only on the Super/Max promo band) became a primary surface?**
Evidence: the dark navy appears on exactly one band (`section.LlPOq`, computed bg 16,15,62) and nowhere else; a "confident dark Duolingo" is brand-evidenced (the app ships a dark mode) without inventing color. Composition bet, differs by axis. Not selected because amplifying the proprietary display face (candidate 1) is a stronger, more ownable, more brand-faithful B than re-grounding the page dark.

### 7 · Audience-routing reframe — **PARTIAL / not selected**
The page does compress several audiences (learners, English-test takers, schools, parents). Foregrounding the core learner path and demoting the rest is real IA work — but it's largely the same move as improvement #3 (cluster the product cross-sells), so it belongs in A's improvements, not as a distinct variant bet.

### 8 · Motif vocabulary swap — **DISQUALIFIED**
The captured page has essentially one composition primitive (the alternating image+text feature row) and no strong under-represented alternate primitive to promote. Swapping would mean inventing a primitive rather than amplifying a captured one.

---

## Resolution

- **C** (motion / cinematic): candidate **4 · Signature-gesture extension**, register `arrival` — the register that naturally amplifies the pushable-button + mascot gesture through motion.
- **B** (composition / static): candidate **1 · Display-typography amplification** — poster-scale Feather as the structural system. Differs from C by axis (typographic composition vs. motion), satisfying the variant-differentiation contract.
- Two strong, non-overlapping candidates remain after disqualification → three differentiated variants are supportable; no `--two-variants` fallback needed.

# "What if…" candidates — Superhuman

Walked all eight candidates against the captured brand surface.

### 1 · Display-typography amplification — **QUALIFIED → C**
What if Super Sans VF's *variable axes* carried the page structurally and
kinetically, not just at a fixed display weight?
- Evidence: bespoke variable family, weights 460/500/540/600/700 captured,
  `--line-height-display: 1.1`, hero at 64px — but the weight axis is static.
- Serves: **C** (the register `kinetic-display` naturally amplifies type).

### 2 · Photography re-foregrounding — **QUALIFIED → B**
What if the painterly editorial photography breathed at full-bleed scale
instead of sitting behind text and chat cards?
- Evidence: `homepage-tonal-flower.webp` (2560w), "Girl drafting a Superhuman
  manifesto" (676×556) — genuine art-directed imagery, **not** stock.
- Serves: **B** (composition-led; differentiates from C by axis).

### 3 · Live-data promotion — **DISQUALIFIED**
The hero's AI-chat cards are illustrative mockups, not live operational data.
No real-time signal to promote.

### 4 · Signature-gesture extension — qualified but not selected
The floating AI-suggestion card is a signature gesture, but the stronger
signature (the typeface) is already taken by C; extending the card motif would
amplify the very cliché improvement #4 wants to retire.

### 5 · Voice-register pivot — DISQUALIFIED
Voice samples are tonally uniform (confident/aspirational throughout); the
"Becoming Superhuman" manifesto is the only contrast and is too thin to pivot on.

### 6 · Color-ladder re-weighting — qualified, runner-up for B
Deep mulberry `#421d24` and teal `#0c4243` appear only as occasional section
grounds; promoting one to a primary surface is a real composition bet. Held in
reserve — photography is the stronger, more brand-distinct B move.

### 7 · Audience-routing reframe — qualified but not selected
The page compresses four products + Enterprise + Education; could foreground
one. Demoted: routing is product-led, not a clean single-audience untangle.

### 8 · Motif vocabulary swap — qualified, runner-up
Product-tile grid is the heavy primitive; could swap to an indexed editorial
list. Held in reserve behind photography.

## Resolved
- **C** → Display-typography amplification (register `kinetic-display`).
- **B** → Photography re-foregrounding (composition, static).
- B and C amplify **different** traits (photography vs. type) → three variants
  are differentiable by axis, not just intensity. Proceed with A + B + C.

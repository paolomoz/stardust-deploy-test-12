# Improvements — https://superhuman.com/

1. **[dated-pattern]** Hero leans on the 2023-era "AI product" formula — a
   darkened purple-gradient portrait with glassy floating chat-card overlays,
   headline + subcopy + one CTA centered on top. The overlays crowd the
   portrait and split attention from "Superpowers, everywhere you work."

2. **[ia-clutter]** "Your Superhuman suite" shows Mail / Grammarly / Coda / Go
   as four near-identical tiles, then each product repeats below as a full band
   reusing the same eyebrow-h2 + 49px-headline pattern — the page states the
   same structural unit five times. The hierarchy between *the suite* and
   *each product* is flat; nothing signals "this is the overview vs. this is a
   deep-dive."

3. **[contrast-or-density]** On the warm cream ground (`#f2f0eb`) the secondary
   ink runs at ~65% alpha (`color(srgb 0.078 0.078 0.074 / 0.65)`), dropping
   under WCAG AA for small text in places; the trusted-by logo wall
   (Zoom / Expensify / Zapier / Brex / Geico / Rivian) is monochrome and
   low-contrast, reading as filler rather than proof.

4. **[cliché]** Glassmorphic AI-chat cards floating over the hero portrait are
   the single most over-used motif in 2024–25 AI marketing. Superhuman owns two
   far more distinctive assets — a bespoke variable typeface and painterly
   photography — yet the hero leads with the cliché instead.

5. **[missed-opportunity]** The captured photography is genuinely editorial
   (the painterly "Tonal Flower" portrait, "Girl drafting a Superhuman
   manifesto") and the type is a bespoke *variable* family (Super Sans VF,
   weights 460→700) — but both are under-scaled. The portrait sits behind text
   at partial opacity, the manifesto image is a mid-size tile, and the variable
   font's weight axis is never exercised as a design device.

# "What if…" candidates — https://linear.app/

## Picked

### Variant C · Live-data promotion
What if: "What if Linear's operational liveness — issues moving, agents
working, progress counting up — *was* the identity, delivered through
motion instead of a frozen screenshot?"
Cinematic register: `live-systems`
Evidence: `_brand-extraction.json#voice` ("Make product operations
self-driving"), `pages/home.json` hero product mock exposes Activity,
issue states, and "02 / 145" counters — all operational signals frozen
in a static image. The product's claim is real-time state; the page
shows none of it move.
Motion bet: hero-adjacent live operations band — count-up numerals,
status-pulse dots on agent/issue rows, fill bars for progress, a
periodic refresh-sweep across the tiles. The *data* is the choreography;
headings stay still (matches Linear's restraint and live-systems'
refusal of animated headings).

### Variant B · Color-ladder re-weighting
What if: "What if the reserved lime `#e4f222` stopped being a tiny
highlight chip and became a primary surface?"
Captured trait amplified: the lime accent (`#e4f222`), currently used in
1–3 highlight chips only (`palette.backgrounds` weight ≈207k vs the
16M-weight `#08090a` ground).
Evidence: `_brand-extraction.json#palette.accentLime` — present but
under-weighted; the brand owns a loud chartreuse it almost never lets
breathe.
Composition bet: one or two large lime surfaces (an inverted hero band
or a closing CTA on lime), lime as the structural accent on section
markers and the primary CTA. The palette is unchanged; only its
*proportions* shift. Pure composition/color move — no motion — so B and
C differentiate by axis, not intensity.

## Disqualified

- **Photography re-foregrounding** — disqualified: Linear ships zero
  photography; its hero visual is a product-UI mock. Nothing to
  re-foreground.
- **Voice-register pivot** — disqualified: the captured voice is
  uniformly confident/terse/engineering across hero, sections, and
  CTAs. No tonal contrast to lean into.
- **Display-typography amplification** — borderline, treated as
  disqualified for *display*: the heading family is Inter (workhorse,
  not a signature display cut), so amplifying it adds no identity. The
  one characterful face (Berkeley Mono) is mono, not display; its
  amplification is folded into variant A's improvement #5 rather than
  made a structural bet.

## Considered but not picked

- **Motif vocabulary swap** (card-grid → indexed editorial list) —
  strong trigger (the "02 / 145" counters hint at an indexed system),
  natural register `kinetic-grid`. Not picked: `live-systems` is a more
  brand-true cinematic register for an operations tool, and the swap
  overlaps with B's composition move.
- **Audience-routing reframe** (foreground "agents" over "teams +
  agents") — real trigger (the page compresses two audiences) but the
  agent angle already dominates the captured copy; foregrounding it
  further would restate, not amplify.
- **Signature-gesture extension** (extend the product-mock vocabulary
  system-wide) — viable, but C's live-data bet is the stronger, more
  defensible amplification of the same underlying asset.

# PRODUCT — Linear (redesign target)

**Register:** brand.

## Users
Software product teams — engineers, PMs, designers — at fast-moving
companies, plus the AI agents those teams now run alongside humans.

## Product Purpose
The product development system for teams and agents: plan and build
products with AI agents — issues, projects, initiatives, PR/agent
review, and progress analytics in one fast, keyboard-first tool.

## Brand Personality
Confident, terse, engineering-grade. Dark-native, high-craft, minimal.
Speed and precision through restraint. Operationally transparent — the
product is real-time state going self-driving.

## Anti-references
Cluttered SaaS pages with stock photography and multi-color CTAs; soft
rounded "friendly" startup aesthetics; marketing divorced from the
product surface; the now-ubiquitous gradient-on-dark dev-tool template.

## Design Principles
1. Dark-native canvas `#08090a`.
2. One signature accent (indigo `#5e6ad2`) + one loud note (lime `#e4f222`).
3. Show the product; the hero is the real app.
4. Craft through restraint — hairline borders, tight radii, type does the work.
5. Engineering register — Berkeley Mono for labels, Inter for everything else.

Tensions surfaced

brand-review-template.md § Detectors: mechanical triggers over the captured surface
single-accent monotony

One blue does all the chromatic work

The palette resolves to near-black, two grays and a single blue. Faithful and disciplined, but every tile competes on identical chrome, so differentiation rests entirely on the photography. A product photo that underperforms leaves its tile visually inert.

uniform-tile flatten

Equal-weight grid hides the hierarchy

MacBook Air, iPad Air, Privacy, MacBook Pro, Trade In and Apple Card all render at the same 40px/tile scale. The captured page has a clear priority order (iPhone hero → Mac → services) but the 2-up grid renders six tiles as peers, so the eye has no second focal point after the hero.

static-on-arrival

Home grid is motion-silent

With reduced-motion the home is fully static; even live, motion is limited to hover-zoom and one autoplay gallery. The brand's cinematic capability (which it deploys heavily on product pages) is absent from the first surface, so the home undersells the brand's own kinetic identity.

typographic-restraint-ceiling

Display type never gets to perform

SF Pro Display is one of the most refined display faces shipped, yet the home uses it at a polite 40 to 56px in a single weight. The headline is always the product name, never a typographic moment. A large amount of expressive range is left on the table.

region-banner-noise

Consent/region strip pollutes first viewport

Capture surfaced a region banner (Schweiz / Weiter) overlaying content. A geo artifact, but representative: the first thing above the hero is a dismissable interruption rather than brand.

# PRODUCT — Duolingo (current state)

## Register
**brand** — a marketing/landing surface for the world's most popular language-learning app. The homepage is a top-of-funnel acquisition page, not the product itself.

## Users
Prospective learners (free + premium), prospective Duolingo English Test takers, teachers/schools, and parents of young children (Duolingo ABC, ages 3–8). Global, mobile-first, casual-to-committed learners.

## Product Purpose
Convert visitors into app sign-ups by communicating that language learning here is **free, fun, and effective**. Secondary: route to adjacent products (English Test, Schools, ABC, Super/Max premium).

## Brand Personality
Playful, warm, encouraging, science-backed-but-never-stern. Gamified and habit-forming. Mascot-led (Duo the owl). Lowercase, plain-spoken, cheerful. The brand wears its friendliness loudly: bright primary-ish colors, rounded chunky type, character illustrations, and the signature 3D "pushable" button. It is confident and mainstream without being corporate.

_Maps to motion register:_ `kinetic-display` / `arrival` — a brand whose identity is built on bounce, springiness, gamified delight, and a tactile pushable button gesture.

## Anti-references
Stern/academic language tutors (Rosetta Stone formality), enterprise SaaS minimalism, muted "premium" neutral palettes, dense text walls.

## Design Principles (observed)
1. **Joy first** — color, mascots, and motion communicate that learning is play.
2. **One idea per section** — alternating image+text rows, each making a single claim.
3. **The pushable button** — the 3D depressible CTA is the brand's tactile signature.
4. **Generous whitespace** — illustrations breathe; nothing is crowded.
5. **Plain, lowercase voice** — "free. fun. effective."

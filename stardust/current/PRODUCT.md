# PRODUCT — Adaline (current state)

## Register

`product` — a developer / ML-engineering tool. The page reads as technical
infrastructure marketing: live trace panels, behavior codes, eval pipelines,
cost-and-latency telemetry. Confident and precise rather than playful.

## Product Purpose

Adaline is an observability and evals platform for self-improving AI agents.
It ingests production traces, groups recurring agent patterns into **behaviors**,
generates **evals** from those behaviors and user feedback, produces
production-faithful **synthetic data**, and surfaces **verified improvements**
a team can approve and ship. Positioned as "the ML layer between your traces
and your agent diffs."

## Users

AI / ML teams shipping production agents — the people responsible for agent
reliability. Buyers span technical (MTS at OpenAI, platform engineers) and
semi-technical (PMs at McKinsey/Lilli, startup CEOs). Used by teams at
Salesforce, DoorDash, HubSpot, Superhuman, Reforge, Discord, Coframe.

## Brand Personality

- **Organic-growth metaphor over hype.** The hero is "Never stop learning,"
  not "10x your agents." Nature photography (mountain valleys, snow ridges)
  carries the emotional register; the product is the calm instrument inside it.
- **Earned-technical.** Real trace logs, real behavior taxonomies, real cost
  numbers. The credibility move is showing the machinery, not hiding it.
- **Quiet confidence.** Muted forest-green-on-warm-paper palette, flat surfaces,
  generous whitespace. No gradients-of-the-month, no neon AI tropes.
- **One theatrical beat.** A single 104px Instrument Serif line at the close —
  "Self-improve your agents now." — is the only flourish on an otherwise
  restrained page.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS gradient hero; neon-purple "AI" cyberpunk; dense feature-grid
landing pages; stock-photo-of-people-pointing-at-laptops.

## Design Principles

1. Show the machinery (live trace / behavior / eval panels) as proof.
2. Keep the chrome quiet so the data and the photography carry the page.
3. Technical labels in monospace; prose in grotesque; one serif moment.
4. Organic palette — paper, ink, sage, forest — never synthetic.

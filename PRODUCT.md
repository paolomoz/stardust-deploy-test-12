# PRODUCT — Duolingo (redesign target)

## Register
**brand** — top-of-funnel acquisition page for the world's most popular language-learning app.

## Users
Prospective learners (free + premium), Duolingo English Test takers, teachers/schools, parents of young children (ABC, ages 3–8). Global, mobile-first.

## Product Purpose
Convert visitors into app sign-ups around one promise: language learning here is **free, fun, and effective**. Route secondarily to English Test, Schools, ABC, and Super/Max.

## Brand Personality
Playful, warm, encouraging, science-backed-but-never-stern, gamified, mascot-led (Duo the owl). Lowercase, plain-spoken, cheerful. Bright color, chunky rounded type, the signature pushable button. Confident and mainstream without being corporate.

## Anti-references
Stern academic tutors, enterprise SaaS minimalism, muted neutral "premium" palettes, dense text walls.

## Design Principles
1. **Joy first** — color, mascots, and motion say learning is play.
2. **One idea per section.**
3. **The pushable button** is the brand's tactile signature.
4. **Generous whitespace.**
5. **Plain, lowercase voice.**
6. **Prove the scale** — the redesign target adds the social proof the current page omits (this is the one purposive change Mode A permits, sourced from the brand's own "world's most popular" claim in `<title>`).

# PRODUCT — Linear (current state)

**Register:** brand (marketing/landing surface for a SaaS product tool).

## Users

Software product teams — engineers, PMs, designers — at modern,
fast-moving companies, plus (per the 2026 positioning) the AI **agents**
those teams now run alongside humans. The page speaks to ICs and team
leads who already know what an issue tracker is and want a faster,
opinionated one.

## Product Purpose

Linear is "the product development system for teams and agents."
Purpose-built for planning and building products with AI agents:
issues, projects, initiatives, cycles, PR/agent review, and progress
analytics in one fast, keyboard-first tool.

## Brand Personality

Confident, terse, engineering-grade. Declarative statements ("Make
product operations self-driving", "Built for the future. Available
today."). Minimal, dark-mode-native, high-craft. The brand signals
speed and precision through restraint — near-black canvas, hairline
borders, one signature indigo, a single lime highlight, and a live
product-UI mock instead of stock imagery. Confident enough to lead with
a one-line manifesto ("A new species of product tool.") and gradient
display type.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered, busy SaaS landing pages with stock photography and
  multi-color CTAs.
- Light, soft, rounded "friendly" startup aesthetics.
- Marketing pages divorced from the actual product surface.

## Design Principles

1. **Dark-native, near-black canvas.** `#08090a`, confirmed by
   `theme-color`. Everything is built on the dark surface.
2. **One signature accent.** Indigo `#5e6ad2`; a single lime `#e4f222`
   highlight; pastel gradient stops reserved for display text.
3. **Show the product.** The hero visual is the actual app UI, not an
   illustration.
4. **Craft through restraint.** Hairline 1px borders (`#23252a`),
   tight radii (4–6px), subtle elevation. Type does the work.
5. **Keyboard/engineering register.** Berkeley Mono for labels and
   eyebrows; Inter for everything else.
